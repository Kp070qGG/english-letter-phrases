来源：2023年新高考I卷 阅读理解A篇（应用文/广告类）

完整英文原文

Bike Rental & Guided Tours

Welcome to Amsterdam, welcome to MacBike. You see much more from the seat of a bike! Cycling is the most economical, sustainable and fun way to explore the city, with its beautiful canals, parks, squares and countless lights. You can also bike along lovely landscapes outside of Amsterdam.

Why MacBike

MacBike has been around for almost 30 years and is the biggest bicycle rental company in Amsterdam. With over 2,500 bikes stored in our five rental shops at strategic locations, we make sure there is always a bike available for you. We offer the newest bicycles in a wide variety, including basic bikes with foot brake, bikes with hand brake and gears, bikes with child seats, and children’s bikes.

The 2.5-hour tour covers the Gooyer Windmill, the Skinny Bridge, the Rijksmuseum, Heineken Brewery and much more. The tour departs from Dam Square every hour on the hour, starting at 1:00 pm every day. You can buy your ticket in a MacBike shop or book online.

选择题

1. What is an advantage of MacBike? A. It gives children a discount. B. It offers many types of bikes. C. It organizes free cycle tours. D. It has over 2,500 rental shops.

2. How much do you pay for renting a bike with hand brake and three gears for two days? A. €15.75 B. €19.50 C. €22.75 D. €29.50

3. Where does the guided city tour start? A. The Gooyer Windmill. B. The Skinny Bridge. C. Heineken Brewery. D. Dam Square.

答案

1.  B 2. C 3. D

中文解析

第1题：答案为B。 题目问MacBike的优势。根据第二段“We offer the newest bicycles in a wide variety, including basic bikes with foot brake, bikes with hand brake and gears, bikes with child seats, and children’s bikes.”可知，MacBike提供多种类型的自行车。A项“给儿童折扣”文中未提及；C项“组织免费骑行游”错误，文中说的是收费的导览游；D项“拥有超过2500家租赁店”错误，原文说的是“over 2,500 bikes”即超过2500辆自行车，而非2500家店。

第2题：答案为C。 题目问租一辆带手刹和三档的自行车两天需付多少钱。根据价格表，1天（24小时）的手刹三档自行车价格为€14.75，每增加一天加收€8.00，因此两天费用为14.75 + 8.00 = €22.75。

第3题：答案为D。 题目问导游城市之旅从哪里出发。根据最后一段“The tour departs from Dam Square every hour on the hour”可知，旅程从Dam Square（大坝广场）出发。

第二部分：阅读理解 B 篇

来源：2023年新高考I卷 阅读理解B篇（人物故事/记叙文）

完整英文原文

When John Todd was a child, he loved to explore the woods around his house, observing how nature solved problems. A dirty stream, for example, often became clear after flowing through plants and along rocks where tiny creatures lived.

When he got older, John started to wonder if this process could be used to clean up the messes people were making. After studying agriculture, medicine, and fisheries in college, John went back to observing nature and asking questions. Why can certain plants trap harmful bacteria? Which kinds of fish can eat cancer-causing chemicals? With the right combination of animals and plants, he figured, maybe he could clean up waste the way nature did. He decided to build what he would later call an eco-machine.

The task John set for himself was to remove harmful substances from some sludge. First, he constructed a series of clear fiberglass tanks connected to each other. Then he went around to local ponds and streams and brought back some plants and animals. He placed them in the tanks and waited. Little by little, these different kinds of life got used to one another and formed their own ecosystem. After a few weeks, John added the sludge.

He was amazed at the results. The plants and animals in the eco-machine took the sludge as food and began to eat it! Within weeks, it had all been digested, and all that was left was pure water.

Over the years, John has taken on many big jobs. He developed a greenhouse-like facility that treated sewage from 1,600 homes in South Burlington. He also designed an eco-machine to clean canal water in Fuzhou, a city in southeast China.

“Ecological design” is the name John gives to what he does. “Life on Earth is kind of a box of spare parts for the inventor,” he says. “You put organisms in new relationships and observe what’s happening. Then you let these new systems develop their own ways to self-repair.”

选择题

4. What can we learn about John from the first two paragraphs? A. He was fond of traveling. B. He enjoyed being alone. C. He had an inquiring mind. D. He longed to be a doctor.

5. Why did John put the sludge into the tanks? A. To feed the animals. B. To build an ecosystem. C. To protect the plants. D. To test the eco-machine.

6. What is the author’s purpose in mentioning Fuzhou? A. To review John’s research plans. B. To show an application of John’s idea. C. To compare John’s different jobs. D. To erase doubts about John’s invention.

7. What is the basis for John’s work? A. Nature can repair itself. B. Organisms need water to survive. C. Life on Earth is diverse. D. Most tiny creatures live in groups.

答案

4.  C 5. D 6. B 7. A

中文解析

第4题：答案为C。 题目问从文章前两段可以了解John的什么特点。第一段描述John小时候喜欢探索树林、观察大自然如何解决问题，第二段描述他长大后不断提出疑问（“John started to wonder…”、“John went back to observing nature and asking questions”），说明他具有强烈的好奇心和探究精神。A项“喜欢旅行”、B项“享受独处”、D项“渴望成为医生”文中均未体现。

第5题：答案为D。 题目问John为什么把污泥放入水箱。第三段说John给自己设定的任务是“remove harmful substances from some sludge”（从污泥中去除有害物质），然后他建造了eco-machine（生态机器），放入动植物，几周后将污泥加入。根据第四段描述生态机器成功消化了污泥的结果，可知加入污泥是为了测试生态机器的净化效果。

第6题：答案为B。 题目问作者提到Fuzhou的目的是什么。第五段列举了John多年来承接的大型项目，包括“designed an eco-machine to clean canal water in Fuzhou”，这是为了展示John的生态设计理念的实际应用。A项“回顾研究计划”、C项“比较不同工作”、D项“消除对发明的怀疑”均不符合语境。

第7题：答案为A。 题目问John工作的基础是什么。文章最后一段John说“You put organisms in new relationships and observe what’s happening. Then you let these new systems develop their own ways to self-repair.”（让新系统自行发展自我修复的方式），这正是“Nature can repair itself”（自然可以自我修复）的体现，也是他整个生态设计工作的核心理念。

第三部分：阅读理解 C 篇

来源：2023年新高考I卷 阅读理解C篇（科普说明文）

完整英文原文

The goal of this book is to make the case for digital minimalism, including a detailed exploration of what it asks and why it works, and then to teach you how to adopt this philosophy if you decide it’s right for you. To do so, I divided the book into two parts.

In part one, I describe the philosophical foundations of digital minimalism, starting with an examination of the forces that are making so many people’s digital lives increasingly intolerable, before moving on to a detailed discussion of the digital minimalism philosophy.

Part one concludes by introducing my suggested method for adopting this philosophy: the digital declutter. This process requires you to step away from optional online activities for thirty days. At the end of the thirty days, you will then add back a small number of carefully chosen online activities that you believe will provide massive benefits to the things you value.

In the final chapter of part one, I’ll guide you through carrying out your own digital declutter. In doing so, I’ll draw on an experiment I ran in 2018 in which over 1,600 people agreed to perform a digital declutter. You’ll hear these participants’ stories and learn what strategies worked well for them, and what traps they encountered that you should avoid.

The second part of this book takes a closer look at some ideas that will help you cultivate a sustainable digital minimalism lifestyle. In these chapters, I examine issues such as the importance of solitude and the necessity of cultivating high-quality leisure to replace the time most now spend on mindless device use. Each chapter concludes with a collection of practices, which are designed to help you act on the big ideas of the chapter. You can view these practices as a toolbox meant to aid your efforts to build a minimalist lifestyle that works for your particular circumstances.

选择题

8. What is the book aimed at? A. Teaching critical thinking skills. B. Advocating a simple digital lifestyle. C. Solving philosophical problems. D. Promoting the use of a digital device.

9. What does the underlined word “declutter” in paragraph 3 mean? A. Clear-up. B. Add-on. C. Check-in. D. Take-over.

10. What is presented in the final chapter of part one? A. Theoretical models. B. Statistical methods. C. Practical examples. D. Historical analyses.

11. What does the author suggest readers do with the practices offered in part two? A. Use them as needed. B. Recommend them to friends. C. Evaluate their effects. D. Identify the ideas behind them.

答案

8.  B 9. A 10. C 11. A

中文解析

第8题：答案为B。 题目问这本书的目的是什么。第一段明确说“The goal of this book is to make the case for digital minimalism”（这本书的目的是为数字极简主义做论证），即倡导一种简单的数字生活方式。A项“教授批判性思维技巧”、C项“解决哲学问题”、D项“促进数字设备的使用”均与文章主旨不符。

第9题：答案为A。 题目问第三段中划线词“declutter”的意思。第三段说“the digital declutter”要求人们“step away from optional online activities for thirty days”（远离可选的线上活动30天），即对自己的线上活动进行“清理”，故declutter意为“Clear-up”（清理、整理）。declutter由前缀de-（去除）+ clutter（杂乱）构成，构词法也印证了此义。

第10题：答案为C。 题目问第一部分最后一章呈现了什么内容。第四段说“I’ll draw on an experiment I ran in 2018 in which over 1,600 people agreed to perform a digital declutter. You’ll hear these participants’ stories and learn what strategies worked well for them”（你会听到参与者的故事，了解哪些策略对他们有效），这些都是实践案例。

第11题：答案为A。 题目问作者建议读者如何使用第二部分中的实践方法。最后一段说“You can view these practices as a toolbox meant to aid your efforts to build a minimalist lifestyle that works for your particular circumstances”（你可以把这些实践看作工具箱，帮助你建立适合自己特定情况的极简生活方式），即建议读者根据个人需要灵活选用。

第四部分：阅读理解 D 篇

来源：2023年新高考I卷 阅读理解D篇（科普/社会议论文）

完整英文原文

On March 7, 1907, the English statistician Francis Galton published a paper which illustrated what has come to be known as the “wisdom of crowds” effect. The experiment of estimation he conducted showed that in some cases, the average of a large number of independent estimates could be quite accurate. This effect capitalizes on the fact that when people make errors, those errors aren’t always the same. Some people will tend to overestimate, and some to underestimate. When enough of these errors are averaged together, they cancel each other out, resulting in a more accurate estimate. If people are similar and tend to make the same errors, then their errors won’t cancel each other out. In more technical terms, the wisdom of crowds requires that people’s estimates be independent. If for whatever reasons, people’s errors become correlated or dependent, the accuracy of the estimate will go down.

But a new study led by Joaquin Navajas offered an interesting twist on this classic phenomenon. The key finding of the study was that when crowds were further divided into smaller groups that were allowed to have a discussion, the averages from these groups were more accurate than those from an equal number of independent individuals. For instance, the average obtained from the estimates of four discussion groups of five was significantly more accurate than the average obtained from 20 independent individuals.

In a follow-up study with 100 university students, the researchers tried to get a better sense of what the group members actually did in their discussion. Did they tend to go with those most confident about their estimates? Did they follow those least willing to change their minds? This happened some of the time, but it wasn’t the dominant response. Most frequently, the groups reported that they “shared arguments and reasoned together.” Somehow, these arguments and reasoning resulted in a global reduction in error.

Although the studies led by Navajas have limitations and many questions remain, the potential implications of the research for group discussion and decision-making are enormous.

选择题

12. What is paragraph 2 of the text mainly about? A. The methods of estimation. B. The underlying logic of the effect. C. The causes of people’s errors. D. The design of Galton’s experiment.

13. Navajas’ study found that the average accuracy could increase even if ____________. A. the crowds were relatively small B. there were occasional underestimates C. individuals did not communicate D. estimates were not fully independent

14. What did the follow-up study focus on? A. The size of the groups. B. The dominant members. C. The discussion process. D. The individual estimates.

15. What is the author’s attitude toward Navajas’ studies? A. Unclear. B. Dismissive. C. Doubtful. D. Approving.

答案

12. B 13. D 14. C 15. D

中文解析

第12题：答案为B。 题目问第二段主要讲什么。第二段解释了“群体智慧”效应背后的运作机制：当人们犯错时，错误不总是一致的，有些人高估有些低估，大量独立估计的平均值会使错误相互抵消。整段都在解释该效应背后的内在逻辑，而非估计方法（A）、错误原因（C）或Galton实验的设计（D）。

第13题：答案为D。 题目问Navajas的研究发现即使在什么情况下，平均准确性仍能提高。第一段指出经典的“群体智慧”要求估计必须独立（independent），而Navajas的研究发现将人群分成允许讨论的小组后，各组估计的平均值比同等数量的独立个体更准确。这意味着即使估计不再完全独立（讨论导致相互影响），平均准确性仍可提升。

第14题：答案为C。 题目问后续研究的重点是什么。第三段开头说“the researchers tried to get a better sense of what the group members actually did in their discussion”（研究者试图更好地了解小组成员在讨论中实际做了什么），并提到最常见的模式是“shared arguments and reasoned together”（共享论据、一起推理），说明后续研究关注的是讨论过程。

第15题：答案为D。 题目问作者对Navajas的研究持什么态度。最后一段说“Although the studies… have limitations and many questions remain, the potential implications… are enormous”（虽然有局限且仍有许多问题，但其对群体讨论和决策的潜在意义是巨大的），这种“虽有局限但意义重大”的评价表明作者持认可态度。

第五部分：七选五（7选5）

来源：2023年新高考I卷 七选五

完整英文短文

Personal Forgiveness

Taking responsibility for mistakes is a positive step, but don’t beat yourself up about them. To err is human. 16 __________ You can use the following writing exercise to help you do this.

In a journal or on a piece of paper, put the heading “Personal strengths.” 17 __________ Are you caring? Creative? Generous? A good listener? Fun to be around? They don’t have to be world-changing, just aspects of your personality that you’re proud of.

At the top of a second page, put the heading “Acts of kindness.” On this one, list all the positive things you’ve done for others. It might be the time when you helped a friend with their homework, when you did the ironing without being asked, or when you baked cookies after the family had had a tiring day. 18 __________

You could ask a friend or family member to help add to your list. 19 __________ That way, you could exchange thoughts on what makes each of you special and the aspects of your personality that shine through. In fact, don’t wait until you’ve made a mistake to try this — it’s a great way to boost self-confidence at any time.

It’s something of a cliché that most people learn not from their successes but their mistakes. The thing is, it’s true. 20 __________ We’re all changing and learning all the time and mistakes are a positive way to develop and grow.

选项

A. A little self-forgiveness also goes a long way. B. Now list all the characteristics you like about yourself. C. They might even like to have a go at doing the exercise. D. It’s just as important to show yourself some forgiveness. E. It doesn’t mean you have to ignore what’s happened or forget it. F. Whatever it is, no matter how small it might seem, write it down. G. Whatever the mistake, remember it isn’t a fixed aspect of your personality.

答案

16. D 17. B 18. F 19. C 20. G

中文解析

第16题：答案为D。 空格前说“为错误承担责任是积极的一步，但不要为此自责。人非圣贤孰能无过”，空格后说“你可以用下面的写作练习来帮助自己做到这一点”。This指代空格中的内容，结合文章标题“Personal Forgiveness”（自我宽恕），D项“宽恕自己也同样重要”承接上文并引出下文。

第17题：答案为B。 空格前提到标题“Personal strengths”（个人优势），空格后列举了“你有爱心吗？有创造力吗？慷慨吗？”，这些都是在引导读者列出自己喜欢自己的特质。B项“现在列出所有你喜欢自己的特点”直接呼应段落主题。

第18题：答案为F。 空格前一段描述“Acts of kindness”标题下要列出为别人做的好事（帮朋友做作业、主动熨衣服等），F项“不管是什么，不管它看起来多小，都把它写下来”总结了前文的列举，强调记录的重要性。

第19题：答案为C。 空格前说“你可以让朋友或家人帮你添加到清单中”，空格后说“这样，你们可以交换想法”，C项中的“They”指代前文的“a friend or family member”，“have a go at doing the exercise”与后文“exchange thoughts”衔接。

第20题：答案为G。 空格前说“大多数人不是从成功中学习而是从错误中学习”，空格后说“我们一直在改变和学习，错误是发展和成长的积极方式”，G项“无论什么错误，记住它不是你性格中固定的一面”承上启下，呼应“错误不是固有性格”与“通过错误成长”的逻辑。

中文解析

第41题：答案为C（made）。 句意：Hough Bakeries在复活节时为克利夫兰所有门店制作小兔子蛋糕。根据语境，烘焙厂“制作”蛋糕，made符合语境。sold（销售）与at Easter time（生产备货阶段）不符。

第42题：答案为A（stores）。 句意：为克利夫兰所有“商店”制作小兔子蛋糕。bakery的产品是送往各个store（门店）销售的。

第43题：答案为D（temporary）。 句意：市中心的工厂在春假期间需要八个孩子做“临时”帮工。春假只有几天，且后文说“three whole days”，说明是短期临时工作。

第44题：答案为A（plans）。 固定搭配“have no plans beyond…”意为“除了……之外没有别的计划”。句意：春假期间我除了听我最喜欢的唱片没有别的计划。

第45题：答案为B（earn）。 句意：我可以“赚”最低工资。earn wage是固定搭配。

第46题：答案为A（worked）。 句意：我想看看工厂是如何“运转”的。work在此处意为“运作、运转”。

第47题：答案为B（permission）。 句意：父母认为这是个好主意，打电话给Betsy的爸爸“征求许可”。call sb. for permission意为“打电话征求同意”。

第48题：答案为C（roles）。 句意：我们在工厂的“职责/角色”很简单。role意为“角色、任务”，此处指工作内容。

第49题：答案为D（Remove）。 句意：把兔子蛋糕从传送带上“取下”。Remove…from…意为“从……移走/取下”，符合流水线操作逻辑——放上去、装饰、取下。

第50题：答案为A（harder）。 句意：这比听起来“更难”。后文提到蛋糕堆积如山、压力很大，说明工作比想象中困难。

第51题：答案为B（Slow down）。 句意：“慢”一点，蛋糕就会堆积起来。Slow down（放慢速度）与后文“蛋糕堆积”构成因果关系。

第52题：答案为C（expected）。 句意：这比“预想”的更有压力。than I’d expected是固定表达，意为“比我预想的要……”。

第七部分：语法填空

来源：2022年新高考I卷 语法填空

完整英文短文

The Chinese government recently finalized a plan to set up a Giant Panda National Park (GPNP). 56 __________ (cover) an area about three times 57 __________ size of Yellowstone National Park, the GPNP will be one of the first national parks in the country. The plan will extend protection to a significant number of areas that 58 __________ (be) previously unprotected, bringing many of the existing protected areas for giant pandas under one authority 59 __________ (increase) effectiveness and reduce inconsistencies in management.

After a three-year pilot period, the GPNP will be officially set up next year. The GPNP 60 __________ (design) to reflect the guiding principle of “protecting the authenticity and integrity of natural ecosystems, preserving biological diversity, protecting ecological buffer zones, 61 __________ leaving behind precious natural assets for future generations”. The GPNP’s main goal is to improve connectivity between separate 62 __________ (population) and homes of giant pandas, and 63 __________ (eventual) achieve a desired level of population in the wild.

Giant pandas also serve 64 __________ an umbrella species, bringing protection to a host of plants and animals in the southwestern and northwestern parts of China. The GPNP is intended to provide stronger protection for all the species 65 __________ live within the Giant Panda Range and significantly improve the health of the ecosystem in the area.

答案与解析

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  题号                    答案                    解析
  ----------------------- ----------------------- -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  56                      Covering                考查非谓语动词。cover与逻辑主语the GPNP之间为逻辑主动关系，用现在分词作状语。句意：该公园面积约为黄石国家公园的三倍。

  57                      the                     考查冠词。固定结构“倍数 + the size of + 比较对象”，表示“是……的多少倍大”。

  58                      were                    考查时态和主谓一致。先行词areas为复数，根据previously（以前）用一般过去时were。

  59                      to increase             考查非谓语动词。不定式作目的状语，表示“为了提高效率”。

  60                      is designed             考查时态、语态和主谓一致。be designed to do sth.为固定搭配，意为“被设计用来……”，主语单数，一般现在时。

  61                      and                     考查连词。连接三个并列的动名词短语protecting…、preserving…和leaving…。

  62                      populations             考查名词复数。population此处意为“种群”，为可数名词，与复数名词homes并列，用复数形式。

  63                      eventually              考查副词。修饰动词achieve，用副词eventually（最终）。

  64                      as                      考查介词。固定短语serve as，意为“充当、作为”。

  65                      that                    考查定语从句关系词。先行词species被all修饰，只能用关系代词that引导定语从句。

第八部分：应用文写作

来源：2022年新高考I卷 应用文写作

写作题目

假定你是校广播站英语节目“Talk and Talk”的负责人李华，请给外教Caroline写一封邮件，邀请她参加节目访谈。内容包括：

1.  节目介绍；
2.  访谈的时间和话题。

注意： 1. 写作词数应为80左右； 2. 请按如下格式在答题卡的相应位置作答。

    Dear Caroline,
    _________________________________________________________________________________
    _________________________________________________________________________________
    Yours sincerely,
    Li Hua

参考范文

Dear Caroline,

I am Li Hua, head of the school radio program “Talk and Talk”. I am writing to sincerely invite you to participate in our program for an interview.

Aiming to promote students’ English proficiency, our program enjoys great popularity on campus due to its casual and interactive style. Many students struggle with oral English, so we would be honored if you could share your expertise and practical tips with us.

We would like to invite you to join us this Friday from 5:00 pm to 6:00 pm, discussing effective ways to improve spoken English. We would greatly appreciate it if you could accept our invitation.

Yours sincerely,

Li Hua

范文中文译文

亲爱的Caroline：

我是李华，“Talk and Talk”校园广播节目的负责人。我写信真诚地邀请您参加我们的节目，做一次访谈。

为了提高学生的英语水平，我们的节目凭借轻松互动的风格在校园里广受欢迎。许多学生在英语口语方面遇到困难，如果您能与我们分享您的专业知识和实用技巧，我们将不胜荣幸。

我们希望邀请您本周五下午5:00至6:00参加节目，探讨提高英语口语的有效方法。如果您能接受我们的邀请，我们将非常感激。

谨启，

李华

写作解析

文体与结构： 本文为邀请类应用文，采用三段式结构。第一段自我介绍并点明邀请意图；第二段介绍节目特色和访谈话题；第三段说明时间并礼貌致谢。

高分表达： - “head of the school radio program” — 高级表达，替代普通的“in charge of” - “Aiming to promote students’ English proficiency” — 现在分词作状语，句式高级 - “We would be honored if you could…” — 礼貌委婉的请求句式 - “We would greatly appreciate it if you could accept our invitation.” — 经典邀请信结尾句

注意事项： 注意时态以一般现在时和一般将来时为主；语气需礼貌得体；字数控制在80词左右，不宜过长。

第九部分：读后续写

来源：2022年新高考I卷 读后续写

阅读原文

It was the day of the big cross-country run. Students from seven different primary schools in and around the small town were warming up and walking the route through thick evergreen forest.

I looked around and finally spotted David, who was standing by himself off to the side by a fence. He was small for ten years old. His usual big toothy smile was absent today. I walked over and asked him why he wasn’t with the other children. He hesitated and then said he had decided not to run.

What was wrong? He had worked so hard for this event!

I quickly searched the crowd for the school’s coach and asked him what had happened. “I was afraid that kids from other schools would laugh at him,” he explained uncomfortably. “I gave him the choice to run or not, and let him decide.”

I bit back my frustration. I knew the coach meant well — he thought he was doing the right thing. After making sure that David could run if he wanted, I turned to find him coming towards me, his small body rocking from side to side as he swung his feet forward.

David had a brain disease which prevented him from walking or running like other children, but at school his classmates thought of him as a regular kid. He always participated to the best of his ability in whatever they were doing. That was why none of the children thought it unusual that David had decided to join the cross-country team. It just took him longer — that’s all. David had not missed a single practice, and although he always finished his run long after the other children, he did always finish.

As a special education teacher at the school, I was familiar with the challenges David faced and was proud of his strong determination.

Paragraph 1: We sat down next to each other, but David wouldn’t look at me.

Paragraph 2: I watched as David moved up to the starting line with the other runners.

高分参考范文

Paragraph 1:

We sat down next to each other, but David wouldn’t look at me. He sat there motionlessly, his head drooping low, overwhelmed by a mixture of embarrassment and self-doubt. I reached out and patted his trembling shoulder gently. “David,” I said softly, “courage isn’t about being the fastest. It’s about showing up and giving your best, no matter what.” He lifted his head slowly, tears glistening in his eyes. “But what if they laugh?” he whispered. “Then you hold your head high,” I replied with a warm smile, “because finishing that race makes you a champion in my eyes.”

Paragraph 2:

I watched as David moved up to the starting line with the other runners. The starting gun echoed through the forest, and they were off — all except David, who swung his feet forward with painstaking determination, his small body swaying with each laborious step. The crowd began to notice. Cheers erupted from the sidelines as students from every school started chanting his name. David didn’t win that race, not by a long shot. But as he crossed the finish line, long after the others, his face broke into that familiar toothy grin — the one I hadn’t seen all day. At that moment, he wasn’t just a runner crossing a line; he was a testament to the power of the human spirit. And I had never been prouder.

范文中文译文

第一段：

我们并肩坐下，但David不愿看我。他一动不动地坐在那里，低着头，羞愧和自我怀疑交织在一起。我伸出手，轻轻拍了拍他颤抖的肩膀。“David，”我轻声说，“勇气不在于跑得最快。勇气在于无论发生什么，都出现在起跑线上，全力以赴。”他缓缓抬起头，眼里闪着泪光。“但如果他们嘲笑我呢？”他低声说。“那你就昂首挺胸，”我温暖地笑着回答，“因为在我的眼里，完成那场比赛就让你成为了冠军。”

第二段：

我注视着David和其他跑者一起走向起跑线。发令枪声在森林中回荡，他们出发了——除了David，他迈着艰难而坚定的步伐，小小的身体随着每一步吃力地摇摆。人群开始注意到他。当来自每所学校的学生开始高呼他的名字时，看台上的欢呼声爆发了。David没有赢得那场比赛，差得远呢。但是，当他在其他人之后很久才冲过终点线时，他的脸上绽放出熟悉的灿烂笑容——那个我一整天都没见到的笑容。在那一刻，他不只是一个冲过终点线的跑者；他是人类精神力量的见证。而我从未感到如此骄傲。

写作解析

情节设计思路：

第一段（与开头语衔接）：通过David的低垂姿态和沉默表现其内心的恐惧与自卑→“我”温柔地鼓励和开导→David被感动，重新鼓起勇气。

第二段（与开头语衔接）：发令枪响，其他选手冲出→David以他特有的摇摆步态艰难前行→观众被他的坚持感动，开始为他加油→David最后一个冲过终点线，但脸上绽放出自豪的笑容→主题升华：真正的胜利在于坚持和勇气。

高分语言亮点： - “his head drooping low, overwhelmed by…” — 独立主格结构，描写细腻 - “tears glistening in his eyes” — 诗意表达，画面感强 - “swing his feet forward with painstaking determination” — 呼应原文细节，前后连贯 - “not by a long shot” — 地道习语，意为“差得远” - “a testament to the power of the human spirit” — 主题升华金句

评分要点： - 词数控制在150左右 - 与原文情境和人物性格保持一致 - 两段之间有逻辑衔接 - 主题积极向上，体现坚持和勇气的价值 - 语言丰富，句式多样，恰当使用描写性语言
